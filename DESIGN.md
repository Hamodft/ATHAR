Preserve the current monumental Arabic name, flat green fields, square controls and Tahoma Arabic text. RTL is the default. The landing page persuades through real interface examples; the interview and preview are task surfaces.

Colors: ink #23483c, paper #f6f7f3, surface #fff, soft #e6ece4, secondary #4d6257, error #922d27. Equal gutters: 28px desktop, 20px mobile. Body 16px, primary controls at least 56px. Keep Arabic letters joined. Do not migrate to React for a styling pass.

Motion: 150ms example feedback, 250ms question continuity, 400ms anchor scroll, 500ms landing entrance. Transform and opacity only for custom tweens. GSAP 3.13.0 with ScrollToPlugin is vendored and bundled in the page, without CDN runtime requests. ScrollTo registers once and uses autoKill. Default content stays visible. No global smooth-scroll wrapper. Repeating closing name stops offscreen, when hidden, with reduced motion, and with its visible pause control. Observers and timelines are disposed before replacing the view.

Transitions.dev: learn-more SVG chevron CSS is installed verbatim, with RTL direction tokens; native controls retain focus and touch feedback. Reduced motion removes travel. Focus ring stays on interactive controls; programmatically focused screen titles do not draw a box.

Validation: Playwright on production Worker preview at desktop, tablet and phone, including reduced motion, repeated navigation, errors, local draft reload, keyboard and long content. Account writes and live AI calls are outside visual validation.
