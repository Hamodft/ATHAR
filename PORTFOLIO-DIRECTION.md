# Professional portfolio template

Reference: https://hamodft.github.io/ahmadalshehri/ (Arabic and English variants inspected on 2026-11-01). This is an additive template for Athar's existing output renderer and editor, not a replacement platform or a copy of the reference owner's content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the user's approved experience read as a complete professional website. A spacious introduction leads to substantive, navigable sections rather than a small CV card.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the reference's deep ink surface, warm metallic accent, generous Arabic typography, portrait frame, and measured separators. Provide a light counterpart and existing forest/navy/sand palettes; preserve Athar's editor vocabulary.

STORY: Understand who the person is, read their actual experience and contribution, inspect their skills and education, and contact them only through information they explicitly published. No fabricated counts, logos, employers or credentials.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An identity navigation bar, a large name and explicitly entered headline, a short excerpt of the approved introduction, section links, and a portrait or typographic fallback. Full introduction below; short content does not require invented filler.

FORM: Precisely specified user reference; no random direction selection. Existing classic/minimal/sidebar templates remain selectable. Native disclosure for long experience details, no animation dependency.

FINISH: Verify the actual renderer and guest design workflow at desktop and phone widths, check private/public contact separation, persistence and empty/long content, run build and tests, and document actual results. No publication of user pages without their review.
